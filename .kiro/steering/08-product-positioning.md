# Steering 08 — Honest Product Positioning & Stitcher's Lab Voice

## Positioning Principle
StitchCut is a **Deterministic, Energy-Based Video Condensing Engine** built for the **Kiroween Frankenstein Category** — stitching together four distinct browser/agent technologies (WebAudio PCM DSP, `fast-check` property-tested interval planning, self-hosted `FFmpeg.wasm`, and an `@modelcontextprotocol/sdk` stdio server) into one cohesive zero-server workflow.

## Mandatory Framing Rules
1. **Engineering Honesty (Never Claim Semantic AI)**:
   - **Never** claim that StitchCut uses LLMs, speech-to-text transcription, or semantic "AI highlight understanding" to pick clips.
   - State clearly and proudly what it actually does: **deterministic 20 ms mono RMS acoustic energy analysis, dead-air excision, and duration-weighted energy ranking under a strict time budget** — delivering instant, reproducible, 100% private cuts with zero API keys or cloud latency.
2. **Subtle "Stitcher's Lab" Aesthetic without Obscuring Clarity**:
   - Use cohesive, tasteful Frankenstein / lab terminology (*"Stitcher's Lab"*, *"Raw Specimen"*, *"Stitched Output"*, *"Dead Air Excised"*, *"Deterministic Energy Condenser"*) paired with crisp technical units (`dBFS`, `ms`, `kHz`, `MEMFS`, `COOP/COEP`) so engineers and judges immediately grasp every control.
